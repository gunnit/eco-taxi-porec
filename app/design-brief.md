# EkoTaxi Poreč - Real Vehicle and Brand Revision

Design read: A direct, tourist-friendly local service site that feels as distinctive and handmade as the real bicycle-sidecar vehicle.
Concept spine: The site moves from silhouette to material detail, letting the real EkoTaxi construction reveal itself as the visitor scrolls.
Delivery tier: cinema
Animation mode: animated-website
Design dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 8, MOTION_INTENSITY 7, VISUAL_DENSITY 3.
Design system: Independent EkoTaxi brand using custom CSS and the shipped scroll-scrub engine.

## Journey
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous people-free product film avoids seams and keeps vehicle identity coherent.
Camera architecture: A. A grounded, steady detail move follows the vehicle in one continuous shot.
Chapter 1 - The EkoTaxi ride: The empty vehicle resolves from leafy shade. Headline: "The EkoTaxi ride." Body: "White passenger sidecar, cream canopy and an olive bicycle." Tags: "Open air", "Bicycle and sidecar".
Chapter 2 - Passenger view: The lens moves across the cream canopy and bench. Headline: "Poreč at street level." Body: "A shaded open-air seat with the city close around it." Tags: "Cream canopy".
Chapter 3 - Distinctive form: The camera reveals the bicycle beside the passenger body. Headline: "One bicycle. One sidecar." Body: "The unusual three-wheel form stays true to the real EkoTaxi vehicle." Tags: "Three-wheel form".
Chapter 4 - Direct call: The shot settles into a closing material detail. Headline: "Call Igor or Toni." Body: "Both numbers are active." Tags: both confirmed phone numbers.
World grammar: Photoreal Adriatic summer detail study with leaf-shadow green, canopy cream, brown platform and olive bicycle tubing. Natural side light, locked exposure, realistic materials, minimal blur, no people, no faces, no text, no logo and no watermarks in generated media.
Mobile framing: Vehicle details remain inside the center 60 percent. A dedicated portrait crop is encoded at 404 by 720.
Delivery budget: All desktop clips at or below 32 MiB. All mobile clips at or below 16 MiB.
The journey enacts the concept spine by moving from the vehicle silhouette into the details that distinguish it from a generic front-loading pedicab.

## Locked identity
Palette: Leaf shadow #172019, EkoTaxi olive #718548, canopy cream #f5f0df, platform brown #6b4938, bench taupe #d7c8a7.
Palette defense: These colors are taken directly from the private vehicle and decal references. Olive is the only interface accent; brown and taupe stay material colors inside imagery.
Type: Outfit for display and body, IBM Plex Mono for compact facts. The outlined Lobster-derived EkoTaxi SVG is reserved for the reconstructed wordmark.
Theme: Deep leaf-shadow green throughout, with cream type and olive actions. No section-level light-theme flip.
Corner language: Soft 14 to 16px surfaces echo the canopy corners; contact actions use compact route tabs.

## Brand reconstruction
Primary SVG: Olive EkoTaxi script, a jumping grasshopper above the wordmark, and an olive line illustration of the passenger sidecar attached beside the bicycle.
Logo wording: EkoTaxi only. No unreadable or guessed slogan copy.
Head mark: Simplified cream rickshaw and grasshopper on an olive rounded ground.
Generated media lettering: none. Logo and phone numbers are applied only in deterministic HTML or SVG.
