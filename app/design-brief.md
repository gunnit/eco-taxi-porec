# Eco Taxi Poreč - Design Brief

Design read: A tourist-facing local experience site for visitors to Poreč, with playful coastal confidence and premium simplicity.
Concept spine: The city unfolds from the passenger seat. The whole page is one continuous bicycle-taxi ride from lively waterfront to intimate old-town stone streets.
Delivery tier: cinema
Animation mode: animated-website
Design dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 8, MOTION_INTENSITY 8, VISUAL_DENSITY 3.
Design system: Independent brand using custom Tailwind/CSS and the shipped scroll-scrub engine.

## Journey
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous 15-second ride is more authentic and seamless than separate scenes.
Chapter 1 - Meet your ride: White canopy bicycle taxi waiting beside the Poreč waterfront. Headline: "Poreč, from the best seat." Body: "Hop in and let the city come to you." Tags: "Since 2010".
Chapter 2 - Feel the coast: The taxi begins moving along a sunlit promenade with the Adriatic opening behind it. Headline: "Sea breeze included." Body: "An open-air ride made for slow views and good photos." Tags: "Open air", "Eco ride".
Chapter 3 - Enter the old town: The route glides into a narrow limestone street, camera orbiting gently around the passenger canopy. Headline: "Every corner has a story." Body: "See the streets that cars miss, without missing the view." Tags: "Poreč old town".
Chapter 4 - Arrive smiling: The taxi settles in a warm square, ready for the next stop. Headline: "Your Poreč story starts here." Body: "Call Eco Taxi and choose where the ride takes you." Tags: "+385 95 858 4045".

World grammar: Photoreal Adriatic summer, white limestone, cobalt sea, terracotta roofs, eucalyptus green bicycle accents, sun-washed coral details. Golden side light from camera-left, crisp air, subtle film grain, polished travel-editorial finish. Background remains lower-detail near copy zones.
Mobile framing: Vehicle, riders, and key landmarks remain inside the center 60 percent of frame. Side edges may crop safely. Mobile gets a lighter 720px encode.
Delivery budget: Desktop film under 32 MiB. Mobile film under 16 MiB.
The journey enacts the concept spine by making scroll feel like forward travel from waterfront to old town.

## Locked identity
Palette: Adriatic navy #12304a, sun-washed coral #e85d4a, salt white #f4f1e8, sea-glass green #6f9d8c, muted sky #a8c9d7.
Palette defense: Coastal colors come directly from Poreč, while coral is the single action accent and avoids generic eco-tech neon.
Type: Outfit for display and body, IBM Plex Mono for compact facts. Sans-serif feels contemporary, friendly, and readable for international tourists.
Theme: Deep Adriatic throughout, with restrained salt-white overlays. No section-level theme flips.
Corner language: Soft 16px surfaces, pill only for compact controls, circular route motifs for ride actions.

## Combinatorial design pick
Theme paradigm: Bold Studio Solid.
Background character: Full-bleed cinematic imagery with soft ambient depth.
Typography character: Expressive display, Outfit/Cabinet-like.
Hero architecture: Massive image-first with restrained type.
Section system: Poster-stacked storytelling.
Signature components: Off-grid editorial, oversized metrics strip, layered image crop frames, hover-accordion slices.
Narrative spine: Journey and waypoints.
Second-read moment: One oversized route ring used once behind the booking block.
Composition anchors: Center-left over film, bottom-left over image, off-grid offset, stacked center.

## Section plan
1. Scroll-scrub journey: full-viewport cinematic story with four semantic chapters.
2. The ride: asymmetric facts rail with verified service details and no card trio.
3. Why Eco Taxi: large route-line statement plus two offset benefit panels.
4. Poreč moments: horizontal photo-strip treatment using film poster crops and no repeated split layout.
5. Booking: oversized circular route-ring composition with direct phone, Facebook, and directions actions.
6. Footer: compact address and business identity strip.
